What to listen for

  1. Click Click + isolated note. You hear only the selected note on the right, with metronome clicks slightly left for timing.
  2. Click Click + stem A, then B. Change their selectors to check all six stems. Find that same pitch and attack, not just the instrument part.
  3. Check the stems where that specific note is audible, then click Save exact-note review. More than one stem is allowed. Wait for the green Saved timestamp. There is no separate acknowledgement checkbox.
  4. If the clean reference is too quiet or you cannot distinguish the note, choose Uncertain / mask. Do not mark it absent.

The isolated cue is re-rendered with the original SoundFont, channel controls, pitch and velocity, then made audible at a fixed peak level. It is a listening aid, not recovered clean audio or a training target. The older clean-family player still contains other notes. Use headphones for low bass notes.

The starting sample selects harder and clearer examples for each family, not a random accuracy benchmark. Review these first, then expand to all notes as needed.
